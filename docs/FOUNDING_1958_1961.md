# NASA formation — separate 1958–1961 prototype

This opening chapter is deliberately **not merged** into the Apollo 1961–1969 simulation. It begins on **1 October 1958** and ends on **25 May 1961**. It tests whether recruitment, research priorities and gradually delegated responsibility create a stronger institutional foundation.

## Launch

The dedicated Windows portable is **PerAspera-1958-Portable.exe**. Put it in a writable folder and double-click it. Python is bundled; its modern interface opens in your default browser. Saves are in `PerAsperaData/saves` beside the executable. Use **Save & settings → Save and quit** to stop its local server. Closing the browser stops live stepping; reopening resumes paused.

From source:

```powershell
python -m per_aspera --founding
```

Or double-click **Launch 1958 Prototype.cmd**. Add `--fresh --seed 11` for a separate new opening. The original default `python -m per_aspera` continues to open the Apollo game. Loading a `nasa_1958` save selects the opening automatically; cross-scenario saves are rejected.

## First playthrough

1. **Daily briefing** introduces the inherited institution. NASA is not a blank organisation: NACA laboratories, facilities and an aggregate workforce already exist. Evelyn Price provides inherited acting cover for discretionary research.
2. **People & organisation** shows six senior posts and eight fictional candidates. Read a candidate’s stated position; optionally commission references ($0.25m / 21 days). Appointment costs $1.5m and takes 30 days, representing mobilisation of an aggregate team, not an individual’s salary. Two staffing packages can run together. Candidates and their actual work remain in the record.
3. **Research priorities** offers four broad questions: scalable launch vehicles, spacecraft/return, human spaceflight research, and tracking/operations. Read a proposal and approve an account limit. The specialists choose the technical packages. You may begin before recruiting specialists, using acting cover under direct oversight.
4. Initially, **direct oversight** returns each research-stage report for a continuation decision. Acknowledge it, compare the observation with the named interpretation, then approve continuation or defer the work. This is approval of the scoped investigation, not a menu of individual technical tests.
5. Once a programme director and the relevant specialist are actually in post, **Delegated authority** can grant bounded autonomy. Teams may then advance the scoped work and routine corrective investigation within its allowance. Alternatively, grant routine autonomy but require adverse demonstration findings to return to your desk. These permissions depend on appointments, not calendar unlocks.
6. Use **Next report** to jump to a work/context boundary, or **Run** for automatic progress. Auto is one game day per real second; 0.5×, 4× and 16× are available. Direct reports and exceptions pause presentation. Routine delegated reports do not require repeated executive sign-off.
7. **Records** keeps appointments, reports, authority changes and causal links. Your own interpretation is separate from measurements and staff judgements. **1961 review** summarises demonstrated evidence, gaps and unfinished commitments.

## What decisions change

- Different specialists affect future work duration, research development and observational precision. Candidate optimism and willingness to challenge affect the reported judgement. Public biographies/references are not numerical access to hidden attributes.
- Appointments do not instantly improve research or rewrite a report. Already contracted packages retain their original lead, expertise and conditions. Replacement waits when that post-holder remains accountable for active work.
- A programme director makes formal delegation possible. Independent assurance has its own post and workload. Without an appointed assurance lead, an inherited aggregate panel provides review at greater cost and time.
- Two aggregate research teams are initially available. A third costs $8m and 90 days. Individual specialists and the independent reviewer still have availability limits.
- Research combines scoped development, analysis and trials. Independent review is a measurement/disposition, not a physical improvement. An adverse review can require corrective work and another review; a favourable staff opinion cannot waive that gate.
- Deferring a programme returns only unassigned authority. Contracted work finishes and remains paid history. Results and completed stages remain recorded; resumption needs funded authority.

## Financial abstraction

The scenario has **$180m of finite illustrative no-year authority**: research $145m, organisation $25m, Administrator reserve $10m. These are selected discretionary prototype work packages, **not historical NASA appropriations, payroll or total agency cost**. There are no automatic fiscal-year top-ups, reputation awards or morale bonuses.

A research charter reserves its approved allowance once. Individual packages assign that reserve without a second charge; completion settles payment. Unused authority returns when a programme’s independent demonstration is recorded or when the Administrator explicitly defers it. The ledger and programme accounts reconcile authorised funds, available allowance, active contracts, payments and releases. Empty reserve does not stop already funded contracts.

Proposals show research-stage costs and nominal serial durations, independent review, and a **$12m corrective/review contingency**. The inherited review panel costs $6m / 70 nominal days; an appointed assurance lead costs $4m / 45 nominal days. Specialist expertise changes actual task duration. Queue, appointment and investigation delays can extend the calendar. Underfunded work stops before commitment and returns a resource exception. Reserve releases require an explicit destination and reason; research and organisation accounts cannot be freely transferred.

## Three truths and research limits

The simulation retains private research capability and problem difficulty. Reports expose noisy instrument/model agreement observations, named staff interpretations and test-scope caveats. These synthetic indices are not flight reliability, historical engineering measurements or a readiness score for Apollo.

The first library contains three scoped stages and an independent review per research area, plus corrective trials. It is not an unrestricted research tree, contractor market, facility simulation, full personnel career system or Mercury-flight campaign. Most posts currently have one candidate; programme and propulsion posts offer alternatives. The cast is fictional, not a reconstruction of NASA’s historical hiring decisions.

## Ending and future handoff

Kennedy’s 25 May 1961 commitment is an external historical development, not a reward for player success. The chapter reaches that boundary even if research is incomplete, funds are tied up or posts remain unfilled. Contracts due after it remain outstanding; they are neither instantly finished nor silently forgiven.

`/api/handoff` exports **per_aspera.founding_handoff version 1**, a public institutional review containing people, authority, research evidence, funding, outstanding work, interpretation and source events. Its `merge_implemented` flag is false. It creates or modifies **no Apollo state**. A later adapter must use the validated private opening save as well as this public review, so staff confidence cannot be mistaken for underlying reality. Mapping to Apollo staff, hardware, research dependencies, funds and time is deferred until this prototype is playtested.

Opening saves use `nasa-1958-*.json`, `current-founding.txt`, their own writer lease and `founding-server.json`. Apollo saves, default resume pointer, writer lease and `server.json` are separate. Both scenarios can run at once on different local ports.

## Validation

```powershell
python -m unittest tests.test_founding -q
python tools/smoke_founding.py
python -m per_aspera --founding-demo --seed 11 --save tmp/founding-demo.json
```

The example policy explicitly recruits a cast, begins with directly supervised work, delegates after appointments, funds research and reaches the final institutional review. This verifies a complete route; it does not prove every staffing/budget strategy is balanced. Tests cover recruitment, frozen leadership, independent review, authority limits, exhaustion, committed work, context, export isolation, save/resume and speed invariance. Windows CI also exercises the actual dedicated portable executable, shared UI resources, command security and Apollo-file isolation.

## Historical anchors

NASA began operations on 1 October 1958 with NACA as its core: [NASA’s institutional history](https://www.nasa.gov/history/65-years-ago-nasa-begins-operations/). The opening’s endpoint is Kennedy’s 25 May 1961 commitment: [NASA History Office](https://www.nasa.gov/history/the-decision-to-go-to-the-moon/). JPL’s institutional transfer and Gagarin’s flight appear as external context, not free player achievements. No historical Mercury success is forced into the player’s research record.
